1. Introduction

Next Move AI, formerly called Fortnightly ("the App", "we", "us"), is a personal goal, habit and task app with an optional AI Coach that suggests your next best move. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, where it goes, and your choices.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information the App Uses

Data stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device using Android's local app storage. It is not uploaded to us, and it leaves your device only as described in Sections 3 and 4:

  • Your life goals and what is important to you
  • Habit names, targets and check-in records
  • Task titles, priorities, due dates and notes, and any place you tag on a task
  • Your personal philosophy, quotes and mottos
  • Quick log entries
  • AI Coach suggestions and your feedback on them
  • Your settings, including calendar settings and subscription status
  • Any AI provider API key you enter (Anthropic or OpenRouter)

Location (optional)

With your permission, the App uses your device's precise location to remind you about tasks at places you tag. If you allow background location, the App can remind you when you arrive at a tagged place even while it is closed. Your location coordinates stay on your phone and are never sent to us or to the AI provider. To show a readable place name and address, the App uses your phone's built-in address lookup, which is provided by your phone's operating system. The name of a tagged place (for example, a building or street name) is included with that task when you use AI Coach.

Calendar (optional)

With your permission, the App reads the calendars on your device, such as Google Calendar synced to your phone, to show your events in the App. You choose how far back and ahead it looks and which calendars it includes. The App only reads your calendar and never changes it. Android's permission prompt may mention access to your calendar in general, because the system groups reading and editing together.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or account details (the App has no accounts)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Contacts, photos or files
  • Payment card details (subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play)

💡 The App has no backend server and no database. We do not receive or store any of your personal data.

3. AI Coach

AI Coach is optional. It runs only when you ask it for a suggestion. It uses either Claude by Anthropic, PBC, or a model you choose through OpenRouter, with your own API key, or with the built-in access included in the AI Coach Pass subscription.

What is sent to the AI provider

When you use AI Coach, the following is sent directly from your device to the AI provider over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • Your life goals and what is important to you
  • Your habits and progress in the current fortnight
  • Your active tasks: titles, priorities, due dates, notes and the names of tagged places
  • Your personal philosophy, quotes and mottos
  • Your quick log entries from the last 3 days
  • Recent AI Coach suggestions and your feedback on them
  • What you enter on the AI Coach screen, such as time available, energy level, where you are and any note
  • If you turn on the calendar and allow AI Coach to see it: the titles, times and places of your events in the range you chose

What is NOT sent

  • Your location coordinates
  • Calendar event descriptions, notes or attendees
  • Your device identifiers
  • Your name or contact details, unless you type them into your own entries

AI providers' privacy practices

Data sent to an AI provider is handled under that provider's own privacy policy: Anthropic at anthropic.com/privacy, and OpenRouter at openrouter.ai/privacy (OpenRouter passes your request to the model provider you select). We encourage you to review them. The App sends requests directly from your device, so we have no access to this data.

Your API key

If you enter your own API key, it is stored only on your device and sent only to the AI provider it belongs to (api.anthropic.com or openrouter.ai) to authenticate your requests. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal data. Your data is shared only in these cases:

  • Anthropic PBC or OpenRouter — only when you use AI Coach, as described in Section 3
  • Google Play — if you buy the AI Coach Pass, the purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental authority

There are no other circumstances under which your data is shared.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored on your device using Android's standard app storage, which other apps cannot read. All network communication from the App uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App (removes all data stored by the App)
  • Clearing the App's data in Android Settings → Apps → the App → Storage → Clear data
  • Removing your API key in the App's Settings

We do not keep any copies of your data, as we have no server-side storage. Data already sent to an AI provider is kept according to that provider's own retention policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Since your data is stored on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You may:

  • Access your data — it is all visible within the App
  • Export your data — using the CSV export in the App's Settings
  • Delete your data — by clearing App data or uninstalling
  • Opt out of AI features — simply do not use AI Coach
  • Control location — do not tag places, or deny or revoke the location permission in Android Settings
  • Control calendar access — keep the calendar off, turn off sharing with AI Coach in the App's Settings, or revoke the calendar permission in Android Settings
