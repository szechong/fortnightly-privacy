1. Introduction

Next Move AI, formerly called Fortnightly ("the App", "we", "us"), is a personal goal, habit and task app with an optional AI Coach that suggests your next best move. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, where it goes, and your choices.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information the App Uses

Data stored on your device

The App is available on Android and as a web app (app.h2math.com). On Android it stores your data in the phone's local app storage; in the web app it is stored in your browser on that device.

The App stores the following on your device using Android's local app storage. It is not uploaded to us, and it leaves your device only as described in Sections 3 and 4:

  • Your life goals and what is important to you
  • Habit names, targets and check-in records
  • Task titles, priorities, due dates and notes, and any place you tag on a task
  • Your personal philosophy, quotes and mottos
  • Quick log entries
  • AI Coach suggestions and your feedback on them
  • People you choose to add (the People feature): their names, roles, your notes about your encounters with them, your follow-ups with them (what and by when), and the profiles the AI Coach writes from those notes
  • Your settings, including calendar settings and subscription status
  • Any AI provider API key you enter (Anthropic or OpenRouter)

Location (optional)

With your permission, the App uses your device's precise location to remind you about tasks at places you tag. If you allow background location, the App can remind you when you arrive at a tagged place even while it is closed. Your location coordinates are never sent to us or to the AI provider. They stay on your device, except that tagged places are included in your own Google Drive copy if you turn on Google Drive sync. To show a readable place name and address, the App uses your phone's built-in address lookup, which is provided by your phone's operating system. The name of a tagged place (for example, a building or street name) is included with that task when you use AI Coach.

Calendar (optional)

With your permission, the App reads the calendars on your device, such as Google Calendar synced to your phone, to show your events in the App. You choose how far back and ahead it looks and which calendars it includes. The App only reads your calendar and never changes it. Android's permission prompt may mention access to your calendar in general, because the system groups reading and editing together.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings → Sync with Google Drive and sign in with your Google account. The App then keeps a copy of your data in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive so your phone, tablet and computer stay up to date. The App asks Google only for permission to this app's own folder (the "drive.appdata" permission): it cannot see or change any of your other Drive files, and the folder is not visible in your Drive file list.

What is copied: your goals, habits and check-ins, tasks (including tagged place names and positions), philosophy, quick log, AI Coach suggestions, People and notes, and the times items were changed or deleted (so deletions sync too). What is never copied: your AI API keys, subscription status, theme, calendar settings and other settings that belong to one device.

The copy goes directly from your device to Google over an encrypted connection and is stored under your Google account, subject to Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive it. Your Google account email is shown in the App's Settings and stored only on your device.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or account details (the App has no accounts of its own; if you use Google Drive sync, you sign in with Google and your email stays on your device). The only exception is Next Move AI credit on the web app, described in Section 3.
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Contacts, photos or files
  • Payment card details (the AI Coach Pass is handled entirely by Google Play, and Next Move AI credit on the web is paid through Stripe)

💡 The App keeps your data on your device (and in your own Google Drive if you turn on sync). We do not receive or store it. The only data our server keeps is the small account record for Next Move AI credit on the web app, if you choose to use it (see Section 3).

3. AI Coach

AI Coach is optional. It runs only when you ask it for a suggestion. It uses either Claude by Anthropic, PBC, or a model you choose through OpenRouter, with your own API key, with the built-in access included in the AI Coach Pass subscription (Android), or with Next Move AI credit (web app).

What is sent to the AI provider

When you use AI Coach, the following is sent from your device to the AI provider over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection — directly, or through our server if you use Next Move AI credit on the web app:

  • Your life goals and what is important to you
  • Your habits and progress in the current fortnight
  • Your active tasks: titles, priorities, due dates, notes and the names of tagged places
  • Your personal philosophy, quotes and mottos
  • Your quick log entries from the last 3 days
  • Recent AI Coach suggestions and your feedback on them
  • What you enter on the AI Coach screen, such as time available, energy level, where you are and any note
  • If you turn on the calendar and allow AI Coach to see it: the titles, times and places of your events in the range you chose
  • If you use People: the names, roles and your notes about the people involved, when you ask the AI to update a profile, suggest who can help with a task, or suggest how to reconnect. Please only add notes about others that you are comfortable sharing with the AI provider.
  • If you use People follow-ups: the person's name and what you need to follow up on with them, by when, and whether it is urgent or important, when you ask the AI Coach for your next move.
  • If you use Decide: the decision you describe, your success target, deadline and limits, your earlier decisions and their results, and your latest reflection, when you ask for options, a plan or a review.

What is NOT sent

  • Your location coordinates
  • Calendar event descriptions, notes or attendees
  • Your device identifiers
  • Your name or contact details, unless you type them into your own entries

AI providers' privacy practices

Data sent to an AI provider is handled under that provider's own privacy policy: Anthropic at anthropic.com/privacy, and OpenRouter at openrouter.ai/privacy (OpenRouter passes your request to the model provider you select). We encourage you to review them. With your own key or the AI Coach Pass, the App sends requests directly from your device, so we have no access to this data. With Next Move AI credit, requests pass through our server on their way to Anthropic, but are not stored (see below).

Your API key

If you enter your own API key, it is stored only on your device and sent only to the AI provider it belongs to (api.anthropic.com or openrouter.ai) to authenticate your requests. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Next Move AI credit (web app, optional)

On the web app you can use the AI Coach without your own key by signing in with Google and using Next Move AI credit (free credit for new accounts, then prepaid top-ups). If you use it:

  • Your AI requests pass through our server to Anthropic. Our server adds our own Anthropic key and passes the reply back to you. We do not store your requests or the replies.
  • What our server keeps: your Google account ID and email address (from Google sign-in, which asks only for your basic profile and email), your credit balance, how much AI you used (number of words processed, cost and time of each request), and a record of your top-up payments (amount, date and Stripe reference).
  • Payments are handled by Stripe, which acts as the seller of record and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). We never see your card details. Stripe tells us that a payment succeeded, its amount, and your email address for the receipt.
  • Our server runs on Cloudflare, which hosts it on our behalf.
  • A sign-in token is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. Signing out of Next Move AI credit, or "Sign out and remove data from this device", removes it from the browser.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal data. Your data is shared only in these cases:

  • Anthropic PBC or OpenRouter — only when you use AI Coach, as described in Section 3. If you turn on "Check recent news" in Decide, the AI provider may run a few web searches about your decision (search words are chosen by the AI from your decision) through its own search service, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google (Google Drive) — only if you turn on Google Drive sync: your data is stored in your own Google account, as described in Section 2. We do not receive it.
  • Google Play — if you buy the AI Coach Pass, the purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Stripe — if you buy Next Move AI credit on the web app, Stripe processes the payment as the seller of record, under Stripe's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the web app and, for Next Move AI credit, our server and its small account record (Section 3).
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental authority

There are no other circumstances under which your data is shared.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored on your device using Android's standard app storage, which other apps cannot read. All network communication from the App uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App (removes all data stored by the App)
  • Clearing the App's data in Android Settings → Apps → the App → Storage → Clear data
  • Removing your API key in the App's Settings
  • Google Drive sync copy: turn sync off in the App's Settings (Disconnect), then delete the copy in Google Drive on the web → Settings (⚙️) → Manage apps → Next Move AI → Options → Delete hidden app data

We do not keep any copies of your App data. If you use Next Move AI credit on the web app, our server keeps the account record described in Section 3 while you use it. To have it deleted, email us from the same Google account's address; we will delete it, except payment records that we must keep for accounting and tax purposes (generally 5 years in Singapore). Data already sent to an AI provider is kept according to that provider's own retention policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Since your data is stored on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You may:

  • Access your data — it is all visible within the App
  • Export your data — using the CSV export in the App's Settings
  • Delete your data — by clearing App data or uninstalling
  • Opt out of AI features — simply do not use AI Coach
  • Control location — do not tag places, or deny or revoke the location permission in Android Settings
  • Control sync — Google Drive sync is off unless you turn it on; you can disconnect it at any time in Settings, and remove the app's access in your Google Account → Security → Third-party connections
  • Control calendar access — keep the calendar off, turn off sharing with AI Coach in the App's Settings, or revoke the calendar permission in Android Settings
